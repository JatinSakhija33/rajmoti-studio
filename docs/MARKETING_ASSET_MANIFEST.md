3. **Budget Tiers**:

   - ₹500–₹1,499 (Statement Keepsakes: Framed Prints 8×12/10×14, Softcover Photo Magazine, Clocks, Cushions)
   - ₹1,500+ (Heirloom Masterpieces: Flush Mount Wedding Albums, Edge-to-Edge Acrylics, LED Translights)
4. **Devotional Darshan Sanctum**: Pre-calibrated sizes with authentic darshan shringar for pooja rooms and mandir walls.
