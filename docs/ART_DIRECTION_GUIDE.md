# Rajmoti Studio — Authentic Product & Photography Art Direction Guide

> **Studio Policy**: Authentic, tactile craftsmanship over synthetic generation.  
> Every product photograph displayed on `rajmotistudio.in` must represent actual materials, mouldings, finishes, and printing techniques manufactured in our Jetpur workshop.

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Physical Authenticity**: Real wood grains (teak, charcoal), crystalline cast acrylic with diamond-polished bevels, textured woven canvas, and calibrated LED translight luminescence.
2. **Tactile Interaction**: Visual previews must communicate weight, depth, texture, and touch before purchase.
3. **Neutral & Elegant Setting**: Use warm, neutral studio surfaces (soft limestone, lime-wash plaster, natural oiled oak/teak) that highlight the gift without distraction.
4. **No Fabricated Customers or Artificial Faces**: Never populate sample albums or frames with generic synthetic AI faces. Use approved client milestones (with written release) or elegant typography placeholders.

---

## 2. Product-by-Product Photography Setup

### A. Photo Frames (Standard, Collage & Inner Sparkle)
- **Angles**:
  - *Primary View*: Orthographic straight-on (0° tilt) to verify aspect ratio (1:1 square, 10×14, 12×18, 16×24, 20×30).
  - *Detail View*: 35° isometric corner macro showing moulding depth, corner joinery, and safe glass clearance.
- **Lighting**:
  - Diffuse daylight from high left (45°) through a scrim.
  - Large white bounce board on the right to prevent harsh shadows inside the frame rebate.
  - Polarizing filter on the lens to control unwanted glare while retaining subtle glass luster.
- **Inner Sparkle Frames (14×20)**:
  - Add a subtle secondary pin-spot at 60° to catch the golden starlight inlay without blowing out the photograph.

---

### B. Ceramic & Magic Mugs (White, Black, Thermochromic)
- **Angles**:
  - 15° forward tilt showing rim, cylindrical curve, and handle attachment.
  - Handle positioned at 90° (left or right) to demonstrate print-safe margins near the handle.
- **Lighting**:
  - Dual strip softboxes on both flanks to create smooth vertical rim highlights that define the cylindrical form.
- **Magic Mug (Cold vs. Hot Protocol)**:
  - Photograph Cold state: Matte charcoal heat-sensitive coating with very faint ghosting.
  - Photograph Hot state: Pour hot water (70°C+) and capture the gradual thermal reveal over 15 seconds.

---

### C. Backlit LED Translights (12×18 & 16×24)
- **Studio Environment**:
  - Subdued ambient room lighting (dim studio room) to showcase internal luminance.
- **Exposure Bracketing**:
  - Capture 3 exposures:
    1. *Light Off*: Shows sleek black anodized aluminium chassis and matte front diffusion film.
    2. *Light On (Warm 3000K)*: Exposes for backlit vibrancy with warm ambient falloff on the mounting wall.
    3. *Light On (Pure 4000K)*: Exposes for crisp white archival transparency.

---

### D. Acrylic Shrines & Bitu Devotional Collection
- **Material Nuances**:
  - Diamond-polished bevelled edges (6mm–10mm thickness).
  - Gold-leaf foil backing.
- **Lighting**:
  - Dark background with edge rim-lighting to make the diamond bevels sparkle.
  - Front soft fill light to keep devotional sacred portraits (Radha Krishna, Srinathji, Ganeshji, Ambaji, Jalaram Bapa) vibrant and serene.

---

### E. Personalised Couple Story Magazines & Photobooks
- **Staging**:
  - Hardcover front view with matte velvet-touch art paper texture.
  - Open two-page spread with realistic page curl and center binding gutter shadow.
  - Flat lay alongside delicate studio styling props (kraft sleeve, silk ribbon, brass pen).

## 4. Temporary Placeholder Protocol

When a new product variant is added before studio photography takes place:
1. Use the official **Rajmoti Neutral Swatch**:
   - Background: `#FAF7F1` (cream) or `#FCFAF6` with subtle material texture.
   - Typography: `Playfair Display` serif title with `Montserrat` category tag.
   - Notice: `"Crafted at Rajmoti Studio · Jetpur · Sample Photography Pending"`.
2. Never substitute stock internet photographs or AI-generated stock mockups.
